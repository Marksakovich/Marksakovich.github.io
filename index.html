06 — Questions

Questions parents ask.

Where do lessons take place?

Online, on [Zoom / Google Meet]. A laptop or tablet with a camera is all you need.

What ages do you teach?

From 6 years old. Younger children work with short, game-based tasks; teenagers and adults with real texts, conversation and exam practice.

Why do you recommend two lessons a week?

Speaking is a skill, and skills fade between lessons. With two lessons a week, new language is practised again before it’s forgotten — that’s when conversation really improves.

Which language are lessons taught in?

In the language being learned, with explanations in Russian or English whenever it helps.

Is there homework?

Yes — short and focused, after every lesson, so practice continues between sessions.
